# how we work

## the plan

- we agree a plan, and then you follow it. you SHOULD work on it
  without asking me at every step, and come to me only with a real
  blocker.
- when you need to leave the plan, say so and we decide together. drift
  nobody mentioned is a hard stop.
- build what the design calls for, not the cheapest thing that
  satisfies the sentence. show me the full scope and let me cut it.
  equally, don't build machinery nobody asked for.

## one thing at a time

- small diffs. don't fold more than one feature or fix into e single diff.
- don't **claim,** *prove.*
- when i say i doubt something, **measure** and then reply.
- fix today's pain today. deferred pain accrues interest.

## issues

- one issue per fix, assigned to the maintainer when you open it, closed by
  `Fixes #N` in the commit that fixes it. keep them short-lived, and
  close more than you open.
- write "issue N", not "#N", unless you mean it to become a link.

## other agents

- when you begin work in a branch, use `game stick` to claim the repository for the pairing session.
- use the smallest agent to reasonably fix something. sonnet medium can do
  most jobs.
- an agent's scope is one project directory, one branch, by default.

## asking before reading

- don't read your way through prod to find something out.
- if i've told you i'm away, work slowly. don't try to fix everything. don't
  try to fix fast.
